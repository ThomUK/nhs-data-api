## Finding: month-on-month change is distorted by non-submission and recoding

| September 2026 | Value |
|---|---|
| Waiting list change, simple difference of totals | **−84,248** |
| Waiting list change, providers submitting in both months | **+4,750** |
| 52+ week change, simple / like-for-like | +532 / +2,928 |
| Unexplained removals (experimental, like-for-like) | 289,490 |

13 providers submitted in August but not September, holding 101,432 patients in August.
Nottingham University Hospitals (RX1, 86,339) is the largest. Five Practice Plus Group sites
disappear and six "PPG Hospitals" sites appear under new codes with similar list sizes (e.g.
NTP15 with 4,951 in August; F0U7A with 4,929 in September). This looks like recoding, not
lost patients. ODS successor mapping should confirm it and join the series.

Care commissioned directly by NHS England (no parent ICB; specialised commissioning hubs and
similar) covers 162,119 patients in September. It is grouped as `NHSE` in the population
views so that every view reconciles to the national total.
