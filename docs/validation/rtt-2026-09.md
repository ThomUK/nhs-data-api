## Differences explained

**Non-English commissioners (`NONC`).** The Full CSV includes patients commissioned outside
England (Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, overseas) under commissioner code `NONC`. NHS
England excludes them from its provider and national figures. They were 30,641 waiting-list
pathways in September, weighted heavily to long waits (336 of the 648 over 104 weeks). All
our measures now exclude them.

**Estimates for missing trusts.** The SPN headlines (7.4m waiting, 65.8% within 18 weeks,
116,353 over 52 weeks) include NHS England's estimates for six trusts that did not submit:
RHQ, RA9, RK9, RNS, RX1 and RW5. We publish submitted data only, so ours are lower. The SPN
medians and 92nd percentiles exclude estimates and match ours.

## Findings

**Month-on-month change is distorted by non-submission and recoding.**

| September 2026 | Value |
|---|---|
| Waiting list change, simple difference of totals | −83,840 |
| Waiting list change, providers submitting in both months | +5,158 |
| Unreported removals, NHS England basis | 377,813 |
| Unreported removals, like-for-like (experimental) | 288,197 |
| NHS England unreported removals *with estimates* | 302,409 |

RX1 and RW5 submitted in August but not September. Several Practice Plus Group sites
disappear and "PPG Hospitals" sites appear under new codes with similar list sizes (e.g.
NTP15, 4,951 in August; F0U7A, 4,929 in September), which looks like recoding. The
like-for-like removals figure is close to NHS England's estimate-adjusted one, which
supports the like-for-like approach.

**Persistent non-submitters are invisible to month-on-month checks.** RHQ, RA9, RK9 and RNS
submitted in neither August nor September, so `new_non_submitters` does not list them. An
expected-provider list from ODS is needed.

**NHS England direct commissioning.** 162,119 pathways (September) are commissioned by NHS
England hubs with no parent ICB. They are grouped as `NHSE` in the population views so all
views reconcile to the national total.

## Update: April to July 2026 loaded

Six months now loaded (April to September 2026, publication dates from the second-Thursday
rule). All files passed internal consistency checks.

- August 2026 unreported removals (252,041) and waiting list (7,158,164) match NHS England's
  overview timeseries exactly, a second validated month.
- **Merger found:** North Bristol NHS Trust (RVJ) stops reporting from July 2026 while
  University Hospitals Bristol and Weston (RA7) jumps from 50,605 to 94,932. RVJ is not in
  the September press notice's missing-trust list, consistent with a merger. This inflates
  the July like-for-like change (+98,567). ODS successor mapping is needed to join them.
- Plymouth (RK9) stops from July and Northampton (RNS) from August, consistent with the
  September press notice. Sheffield (RHQ) and Torbay (RA9) are absent for all six months.
- Nottingham (RX1) rises from 78,304 (July) to 86,339 (August) before missing September.
- Cora Health MSK (NMG, independent sector) is missing in July and doubles from 24,720 to
  46,939 in September; worth checking.
