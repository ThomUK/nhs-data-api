Datasets & measures

Every dataset and measure is defined in a public catalogue. The definitions below are the same ones the API returns in meta.measures.

draft definitions or coverage may still change. experimental measures are excluded unless requested with include_experimental=true.

Organisation reference data (ODS)

organisations · daily · draft · 0 measures

Organisations that appear in the statistics (NHS trusts, independent sector providers, commissioners), with role, sector, address, operating dates, operator and successor links, from NHS England's Organisation Data Service. Used to follow organisations through mergers and recodes.

Consultant-led Referral to Treatment (RTT) Waiting Times

rtt · monthly · draft · 28 measures

Monthly counts of patients on consultant-led elective care pathways in England, by provider, commissioner and treatment function, split into weekly waiting-time bands. Covers the open waiting list (incomplete pathways), completed pathways and new clock starts.

Waiting List Minimum Data Set (WLMDS)

wlmds · weekly · draft · 0 measures

Weekly patient-level collection of elective waiting lists, published as aggregate statistics alongside the monthly RTT data. Gives a more timely view of the waiting list.
