Organisation reference data (ODS)

Organisations that appear in the statistics (NHS trusts, independent sector providers, commissioners), with role, sector, address, operating dates, operator and successor links, from NHS England's Organisation Data Service. Used to follow organisations through mergers and recodes.

Caveats

Caveats are also returned in every response's meta.caveats.

ods-lineage
Successor links come from ODS where recorded (for example North Bristol NHS Trust RVJ to RA7 from 1 July 2026). Changes of operator can issue new codes without a successor record; those links are curated manually with evidence and shown in /v1/organisations/{code} as curated_lineage.
ods-snapshot
Taken from a dated snapshot of the ODS ORD API; names and statuses are as at the snapshot date, not the reporting month.
