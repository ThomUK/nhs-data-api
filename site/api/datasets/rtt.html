Consultant-led Referral to Treatment (RTT) Waiting Times

Monthly counts of patients on consultant-led elective care pathways in England, by provider, commissioner and treatment function, split into weekly waiting-time bands. Covers the open waiting list (incomplete pathways), completed pathways and new clock starts.

weeks_band · Waiting-time band

Weeks waited, as a half-open interval (weeks_lower, weeks_upper]. The final band is open-ended (more than 104 weeks in current files). Two special bands have no weeks: "unknown_clock_start" (completed pathways Part_1A/1B only) and "unbanded" (Part_3 new RTT periods, which are published as a single total with no waiting-time split).

Caveats

Caveats are also returned in every response's meta.caveats.

rtt-english-commissioners
All measures exclude patients commissioned outside England (commissioner code NONC: Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, overseas), matching NHS England's published figures. In September 2026 this was 30,641 waiting-list pathways, weighted towards long waits.
rtt-no-estimates
Figures are sums of submitted data and do not include NHS England's estimates for providers that did not submit. National headline figures in NHS England's press notice do include estimates, so they can be higher (7.38m vs 7.07m waiting, September 2026). Medians and 92nd percentiles in the press notice exclude estimates and match ours.
rtt-persistent-non-submission
new_non_submitters only detects providers that stopped submitting since the previous month. Providers missing for longer are listed in NHS England's press notice and will be detected once ODS reference data defines the expected set of providers.
rtt-independent-sector
Independent sector providers appear as providers in their own right. Totals by NHS trust exclude NHS-funded activity delivered by independent providers unless they are included explicitly.
rtt-revisions
Figures can be revised in later publications. This API keeps every vintage; the default view returns the latest revised figure. Use the as_published view for the figure as first released.
rtt-non-submission
Some organisations do not submit for some months (for example after an electronic patient record go-live). Missing data are not zeros; national totals in the source may include estimates for non-submitters. Organisation-month caveats are listed here as they are identified.
