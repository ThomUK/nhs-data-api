The measure row

One organisation (or area) in one month and one specialty, with the requested measures as columns. Which organisation a row describes depends on the view: a provider, an ICB's providers, an ICB's residents, or England.

Returned by: /v1/rtt/metrics

Fields
perioddate

Reporting month, as the first day of the month (ISO 8601).

org_codestring

ODS code of the organisation or area. ENG for England; NHSE for care commissioned directly by NHS England (population views).

org_namestring

Name as published for that month.

treatment_function_codestring

Specialty. C_999 is all specialties combined. Codes are listed on the RTT dataset page.

waiting_list_totalinteger

Total waiting list (incomplete pathways). Patients waiting to start treatment at month end. Sum of Part_2 across all weekly bands.

waiting_list_dtainteger

Waiting with a decision to admit. Incomplete pathways where a decision to admit for treatment has been made (Part_2A).

pct_waiting_list_dtanumber · nullable

Share of the waiting list awaiting admission. waiting_list_dta / waiting_list_total x 100.

pct_within_18_weeksnumber · nullable

Percentage waiting 18 weeks or less. Part_2 patients in bands with weeks_upper <= 18, divided by waiting_list_total, x 100.

waiting_over_18_weeksinteger

Waiting more than 18 weeks. Part_2 patients in bands with weeks_lower >= 18.

waiting_over_52_weeksinteger

Waiting more than 52 weeks. Part_2 patients in bands with weeks_lower >= 52.

waiting_over_65_weeksinteger

Waiting more than 65 weeks. Part_2 patients in bands with weeks_lower >= 65.

waiting_over_78_weeksinteger

Waiting more than 78 weeks. Part_2 patients in bands with weeks_lower >= 78.

waiting_over_104_weeksinteger

Waiting more than 104 weeks. Part_2 patients in the open-ended top band (more than 104 weeks).

median_wait_weeksnumber · nullable

Median wait (weeks). 50th percentile of the Part_2 waiting-time distribution, by linear interpolation within the weekly band containing the median patient. Same method as NHS England (matched exactly for all providers and specialties, September 2026). Rounded to 1 decimal place.

p92_wait_weeksnumber · nullable

92nd percentile wait (weeks). 92nd percentile of the Part_2 waiting-time distribution, interpolated as for the median. If it falls in the open-ended top band, the band's lower bound (104) is returned. Same method as NHS England (matched exactly, September 2026). Rounded to 1 decimal place.

approaching_52_weeksinteger

Approaching 52 weeks. Part_2 patients waiting more than 40 and up to 52 weeks. Will exceed 52 weeks within 12 weeks unless treated or removed.

clock_startsinteger

New RTT periods (clock starts). Pathways starting in the month (Part_3). Published as a total with no waiting-time split.

clock_stops_admittedinteger

Completed pathways, admitted. Pathways ending with admission for treatment in the month (Part_1A), including unknown clock start.

clock_stops_non_admittedinteger

Completed pathways, non-admitted. Pathways ending in the month without admission (Part_1B), including unknown clock start.

clock_stops_totalinteger

Completed pathways, total. clock_stops_admitted + clock_stops_non_admitted.

pct_completed_within_18_weeks_admittednumber · nullable

Admitted pathways completed within 18 weeks. Part_1A patients in bands with weeks_upper <= 18, divided by Part_1A with a known clock start, x 100.

pct_completed_within_18_weeks_non_admittednumber · nullable

Non-admitted pathways completed within 18 weeks. As above for Part_1B.

median_wait_completed_admitted_weeksnumber · nullable

Median wait of admitted completed pathways (weeks). Median of the Part_1A distribution (known clock starts), interpolated within weekly bands.

median_wait_completed_non_admitted_weeksnumber · nullable

Median wait of non-admitted completed pathways (weeks). Median of the Part_1B distribution (known clock starts), interpolated within weekly bands.

waiting_list_change_mominteger · nullable

Change in waiting list since previous month. waiting_list_total minus the previous month's, as published. Null if the previous month is not loaded or the organisation/specialty does not appear in it. Null when the previous month is not available.

waiting_over_52_weeks_change_mominteger · nullable

Change in 52+ week waiters since previous month. waiting_over_52_weeks minus the previous month's, as published. Null when the previous month is not available.

waiting_list_change_mom_like_for_likeinteger · nullable

Change in waiting list since previous month, like-for-like providers. Change in waiting_list_total counting only providers that submitted in both months. Null when the previous month is not available.

waiting_over_52_weeks_change_mom_like_for_likeinteger · nullable

Change in 52+ week waiters since previous month, like-for-like providers. Change in waiting_over_52_weeks counting only providers that submitted in both months. Null when the previous month is not available.

unreported_removalsinteger · nullable

Derived unreported completed pathways and removals. Previous month's waiting list + clock_starts - clock_stops_total - this month's waiting list, from published totals. Matches NHS England's "Derived unreported completed pathways and removals" (without estimates). Null when the previous month is not available.

unreported_removals_like_for_likeinteger · nullable experimental

Derived unreported completed pathways and removals, like-for-like (experimental). Previous month's waiting list + clock_starts - clock_stops_total - this month's waiting list, counting only providers that submitted in both months. A positive value means the list fell by more than recorded treatment explains. Null when the previous month is not available.

providers_submittinginteger

Providers submitting. Number of providers with any rows in the group for the month.

The meta object

Returned with every successful response. It says where the data came from, how to cite it, what caveats apply, and defines every measure returned, so a response can be used on its own.

Returned by: all endpoints

Fields
datasetstring

Dataset id, e.g. rtt or organisations.

titlestring

Dataset title.

source_pageurl

The publisher's page for the source publication.

licencestring

Licence of the source data (Open Government Licence v3.0).

attributionstring

Text to quote when citing the figures. Attribution is a condition of the licence.

caveatsarray of caveat

Known limitations that apply to the dataset. Pass them on with the figures.

measuresmap of measure definition · nullable

Definitions of the measures in this response, keyed by measure id. Present on measure endpoints.

viewstring · nullable

The view used (measure endpoints).

rowsinteger · nullable

Number of rows matching the request, before any truncation.

truncatedboolean · nullable

True when more rows matched than were returned (the limit is 1,000). Narrow the request.

The measure definition

How a measure is defined, returned in meta.measures. The same definitions appear on the dataset pages.

Returned by: meta.measures

Fields
labelstring

Short human-readable name.

unitstring

Unit of the values.

One of: count, percent, weeks
definitionstring

Exactly how the measure is calculated.

statusstring

experimental measures are only returned when requested with include_experimental=true.

One of: stable, draft, experimental
standardstring · nullable

Related operational standard, e.g. the 92% 18-week standard.

caveatstring · nullable

Measure-specific caveat.

Example
{
  "label": "Percentage waiting 18 weeks or less",
  "unit": "percent",
  "definition": "Part_2 patients in bands with weeks_upper <= 18, divided by waiting_list_total, x 100.",
  "standard": "NHS Constitution operational standard of 92%.",
  "status": "stable"
}

The organisation

An organisation that appears in the statistics, from NHS England's Organisation Data Service (ODS), with the links used to follow it through mergers and recodes.

Returned by: /v1/organisations, /v1/organisations/{org_code}

Fields
org_codestring

ODS code.

namestring

Name in the ODS snapshot.

sectorstring

Derived from the ODS primary role.

One of: nhs_trust, independent_sector, commissioner, other
primary_rolestring

ODS primary role code, e.g. RO197 (NHS trust).

primary_role_namestring

Name of the primary role.

statusstring

ODS status (Active or Inactive).

operational_startdate · nullable

Date the organisation became operational.

operational_enddate · nullable

Date it stopped operating, if any.

legal_startdate · nullable

Legal start date, where recorded.

legal_enddate · nullable

Legal end date, where recorded (e.g. 30 June 2026 for a trust merged on 1 July).

closed_ondate · nullable

Earliest of operational_end and legal_end.

postcodestring · nullable

Postcode of the main address.

address_line1string · nullable

First line of the address.

townstring · nullable

Town.

operated_bystring · nullable

ODS code of the operating organisation, for sites.

successorsarray of string

ODS codes of successor organisations recorded by ODS.

predecessorsarray of string

ODS codes of predecessor organisations recorded by ODS.

curated_lineagearray of lineage link · nullable

Links ODS does not record (for example a change of operator issuing new codes), confirmed with evidence. Single-organisation endpoint only.

The non-submitter

A provider that submitted last month but not this month, with the likely reason.

Returned by: submission_record.new_non_submitters

Fields
codestring

ODS code.

namestring

Name as last published.

reasonstring

merged_or_recoded: its successor is submitting. closed: ODS records it as closed. not_submitting: usually a missed submission.

One of: merged_or_recoded, closed, not_submitting
successorstring · nullable

Successor code, when merged or recoded.

closed_ondate · nullable

Closure date from ODS, when closed.

Example
{
  "code": "D1V5N",
  "name": "ACES BOLTON",
  "reason": "not_submitting",
  "successor": null,
  "closed_on": null
}
