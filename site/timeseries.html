Flag rules

  • Missing month: no value, although earlier and later months have values (often a missed submission).
  • No data this month: the latest month has no value but earlier months do (a missed submission, merger, recoding or closure).
  • Jump: change on the previous month of at least the threshold, as a percentage for counts (ignored below 20) or in points/weeks for percentages and waits. Not applied to month-on-month change measures, which cross zero.
  • Unusual change: with at least 6 earlier month-on-month changes, this month's change is more than 3.5 robust standard deviations (scaled median absolute deviation) from the median change of the previous 12 months. A step change is flagged in the month it happens, not every month after.
